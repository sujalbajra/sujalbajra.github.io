Background

About

I am a final-year B.Tech Artificial Intelligence student at Kathmandu University, graduating in 2027, with a cumulative GPA of 4.0. My research centres on temporal graph learning and low-resource NLP — specifically, detecting and predicting emerging research communities in large-scale dynamic scholarly graphs.

I am also drawn to the problem of building language technology for underrepresented languages. Two of my ongoing papers directly address this: one creates the first standardised IR benchmark for Nepal Bhasa, and another builds the first community-sourced character dataset for seven ancient Nepali scripts.

Outside research, I lead student teams as RnD Lead of KU AI Club and coordinate cloud infrastructure workshops as an Executive Member of the AWS Student Builder Group at KU.

Published

AI-Powered Stock Forecasting: A Graph-Based Approach for NEPSE

Journal of NICS · NepJOL · 2025

Published

Developed a visibility-based graph transformation technique that converts financial time-series into structured networks capturing long-memory dependencies. Benchmarked GCN and GAT against standard baselines for price-movement prediction on NEPSE. GCN demonstrated superior robustness in modelling complex financial relationships, outperforming both GAT and non-graph methods across all evaluation metrics.

Key techniques

EmerG-Net: Community Detection & Trend Prediction on Dynamic Scholarly Graphs

In Progress

Processed the full OpenAlex S3 dump (~2 TB) to build a MeSH biomedical citation graph with PubMed abstracts. Reframed emerging-trend prediction as a ranking problem. Community detection via HiT-Leiden; temporal scoring via TGN. Targeting NeurIPS, KDD, or ICML.

NDCG >95% ~2 TB dataset TGN + HiT-Leiden

Information Retrieval for Nepal Bhasa (Newari)

In Progress

Built the first standardised IR benchmark for Nepal Bhasa — 80K documents, custom Devanagari preprocessing, and 1,000 annotated query-document pairs. Evaluated mBERT, MuRIL, GTE and fine-tuned variants. Also produced a 15K-word POS-tagged corpus in CoNLL-U format.

MAP 0.83 Recall@10 >0.89 +46.5% vs TF-IDF
GitHub ↗

Nepali Lipi: Few-Shot OCR for Ancient Nepali Scripts

Manuscript Ready

Community-sourced dataset of 8,200 images across 328 classes covering seven ancient Nepali scripts (Brahmi, Bhujimol, Lichhavi, Nandinagari, Tibetan, Kirat, Maithali). Evaluated Prototypical Networks, Laplacian-Shot, Relation Networks, SimpleShot on ResNet-12.

5-shot Acc 95.38% 8,200 images 7 scripts
