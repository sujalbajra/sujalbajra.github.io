§1 Background

About

I am a final-year B.Tech Artificial Intelligence student at Kathmandu University, graduating in 2027, with a cumulative GPA of 4.0. My research centres on temporal graph learning and low-resource NLP — specifically, detecting and predicting emerging research communities in large-scale dynamic scholarly graphs.

I'm also drawn to the problem of building language technology for underrepresented languages. Two of my ongoing papers address this directly: one creates the first standardised IR benchmark for Nepal Bhasa, and another builds the first community-sourced character dataset for seven ancient Nepali scripts.

Outside research, I lead student teams as R&D Lead of KU AI Club and coordinate cloud infrastructure workshops as an Executive Member of the AWS Student Builder Group at KU.

Nepali Lipi: Few-Shot OCR for Ancient Nepali Scripts

Preprint ready — first community-sourced dataset for seven ancient scripts

Community-sourced dataset of 8,200 images across 328 classes covering Brahmi, Bhujimol, Lichhavi, Nandinagari, Tibetan, Kirat, and Maithali scripts. Evaluated Prototypical Networks, Laplacian-Shot, Relation Networks, and SimpleShot on ResNet-12.
